# Developer utility growth

## Contract

| Field | Bound contract |
|---|---|
| Trigger | User asks for a free-tool, generator, checklist, or ungated-resource plan tied to the product. |
| Authority | Read-only brief in chat. No tool build, deployment, form collection, hidden gate, forced account, or tracking credential. |
| Side effect | A resource brief with gating, privacy, attribution, and success-signal decisions. Nothing built or collected. |
| Done | User problem, instant value, shareability, product relationship, no-gate or soft-gate choice with reasons, privacy implications, attribution limits, and success signals are all explicit. |

## Not for

- Lead capture machinery, enrichment, or forced signup funnels. Any gate is explicit, minimal, and justified in the brief.

## Inputs

- `problem` (required): the developer problem the free resource solves in one session.
- `product` (required): how the resource relates to the paid or core product without becoming a demo wall.
- `constraints` (optional): hosting, maintenance, or brand limits the user names.

## Procedure

1. State the instant value: what the user gets in the first session with no account. **Done when:** the first-session outcome is concrete and account-free.
2. Map shareability: what artifact, link, or output the user naturally passes on. **Done when:** the shareable unit is named.
3. Draw the product relationship: adjacent help that earns trust, not a crippled product slice. **Done when:** the boundary between free value and product value is written.
4. Decide gating openly: no-gate or soft-gate, with reasons, data asked, and privacy implications. Hidden gates and forced accounts are refused. **Done when:** the gate decision states what is asked, why, and the privacy cost.
5. Set attribution limits: how far brand and links go without polluting the output. **Done when:** attribution rules are explicit.
6. Define success signals from observable behavior (repeat use, shares, downstream product trials the user can already measure). **Done when:** each signal names its existing measurement source.

## Failure and recovery

- No account-free instant value exists: say so and reshape the concept; do not hide the product behind a form.
- Maintenance cost exceeds the stated constraints: name the cut that fits or stop.

## Output

A resource brief: problem, instant value, shareable unit, product boundary, gate decision with privacy notes, attribution rules, success signals, and open risks.
