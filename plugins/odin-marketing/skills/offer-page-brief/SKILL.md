# Offer page brief

## Contract

| Field | Bound contract |
|---|---|
| Trigger | User supplies one offer, one primary audience, and one principal action. |
| Authority | Read-only. Blueprint and production requirements in chat. No page implementation, publication, or final-copy delivery. |
| Side effect | A page blueprint the user can hand to `copywriting` or an approved design owner. Nothing built or published. |
| Done | Offer map is complete, one internal proposition is formulated, every page block answers a distinct buying question, visuals are evidence-bearing, terms and next step are explicit, and no testimonial, result, urgency, scarcity, guarantee, price, or competitor claim is invented. |

## Not for

- Writing final page copy, building the page, or publishing it.

## Inputs

- `offer` (required): category, deliverable, features and mechanisms, price and terms.
- `audience` (required): one primary audience in one situation.
- `action` (required): the one principal action the page asks for.
- `proof` (required): supplied evidence, approved comparisons, available visual assets, eligibility and fit limits.

## Procedure

1. Build the offer map: category and deliverable; audience and situation; features and mechanisms; supported consequences; proof; price and terms; eligibility and fit limits; approved comparisons; available visuals; missing support. **Done when:** all ten slots are filled or explicitly marked missing.
2. Formulate one internal proposition from supported consequences only. **Done when:** the proposition contains no claim missing from the proof slot.
3. Select only page blocks that answer distinct buying questions (fit, mechanism, proof, terms, risk, next step). Drop decorative blocks. **Done when:** each block names the buying question it answers.
4. Require evidence-bearing visuals: every visual instruction states what evidence it shows, never decoration. **Done when:** each visual has an evidence caption requirement.
5. Make transaction terms and the actual next step explicit, including limits and exclusions. **Done when:** price, terms, eligibility, and next step are all stated in plain terms.
6. Verify: no invented testimonials, results, urgency, scarcity, guarantees, prices, or competitor claims. Flag truthful-urgency conditions (real deadlines, real capacity) only with their proof. **Done when:** every persuasion element traces to proof or is removed.

## Failure and recovery

- Offer facts or proof missing: produce the blueprint skeleton with the missing support flagged as production blockers; do not fill gaps with invention.
- Multiple audiences or actions: split into separate briefs.

## Output

A page blueprint: offer map, internal proposition, ordered page path with per-block buying questions, visual evidence requirements, terms and CTA specification, missing-support list, and the verification record.
